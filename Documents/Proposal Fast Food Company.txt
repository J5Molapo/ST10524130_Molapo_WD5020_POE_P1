1. Project Objective & Permission Request
I am submitting this proposal to request formal approval to approach Angry Bob's Pizza, owned by Holly Terry, as part of my Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) practical module. Angry Bob's Pizza has been in business for 8 years and has built a strong reputation for serving fresh, affordable, and flavour-packed pizza to a loyal customer base. The goal of this project is to create a modern, responsive website that presents the brand professionally, improves online ordering visibility, and helps the business attract new customers while keeping existing customers engaged.
2. Goals and Objectives
* The website design aims to improve customer experience by making the menu, pricing, delivery, and contact information easy to find.
* Online ordering and enquiry features will make it easier for customers to place orders and book catering or group deals.
* Visual identity and branding will be improved so that Angry Bob's Pizza looks established, professional, and memorable.
* The website will help communicate the business's 8 years of experience and its reputation for quality food and customer service.
3. Current Business Analysis (Angry Bob's Pizza)
* Limited Digital Presence: The business has a strong local reputation, but an outdated or weak online presence may reduce visibility and customer reach.
* Lack of Online Ordering Convenience: Customers may need a more efficient way to view the menu and place orders without calling or visiting in person.
* Weak Brand Presentation: Without a strong digital identity, the business may not stand out from other local fast food competitors.
* Inconsistent Information: Key details such as menu items, contact information, delivery areas, and operating hours may not be presented clearly.
4. Proposed Essentials (UX, Design & Business Features)
* Responsive Layout & Modern Aesthetics: A clean, mobile-friendly website that looks professional on phones, tablets, and desktops.
* Menu Showcase: Clear categories such as Classic Pizzas, Gourmet Pizzas, Family Combos, Sides, Drinks, and Desserts.
* Online Ordering & Enquiry Forms: Customers can place custom pizza orders, enquire about catering, or book deliveries.
* Delivery & Collection Information: Easy access to delivery zones, order minimums, and store collection details.
* Promotions & Loyalty Section: Highlight daily specials, combo deals, and loyalty rewards for returning customers.
* About the Business Section: Featuring Angry Bob's Pizza's 8 years of experience, customer focus, and quality food offerings.
* Accessibility & Speed: A faster, more user-friendly website with readable text, clear navigation, and simple layout.
* Brand Colour Palette: Warm reds, dark charcoal, cream, and white to reflect energy, appetite appeal, and a strong fast-food identity.
* Typography: Bold, modern fonts that feel energetic and food-focused while staying easy to read.
5. Technical Requirements
* Frontend Stack: HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript for a lightweight, responsive, and user-friendly experience.
* Contact & Enquiry Forms: Functional forms for customer queries, catering requests, and delivery bookings.
* Product Display Pages: A dedicated menu section with pricing and product descriptions.
* SEO & Optimization: Search-friendly page titles, readable content, and optimized image use for faster loading.
* Mobile-First Design: Ensuring easy browsing and ordering on mobile devices, where many customers order food.
